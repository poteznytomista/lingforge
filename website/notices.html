Notices & privacy.

This website

The site has no account, analytics, tracking pixel, advertising or remote font request. Your motion preference is saved locally in your browser when storage is available. The clipboard is written only when you select Copy checksum; clipboard contents are never read.

GitHub Pages hosts the published site and GitHub Releases serves its AppImage download. Those services process requests under their own policies. This site does not promise that the hosting provider keeps no access logs. Read GitHub’s privacy statement.

Type and artwork

The site uses locally bundled Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono, distributed under their included SIL Open Font License notices:

  • Space Grotesk license
  • IBM Plex Mono license

Workspace examples, the name Avelin, its words and the illustrated glyphs are presentation examples. They are not included project data, real user projects or screenshots of a production-ready editor.

The application

LingForge 0.1.0 is an experimental development build. Windows and full release acceptance remain unfinished. This website does not change the application’s license, grant rights to imported artwork, or claim a completed audit of every redistributed dependency. Application identity, licensing and signing must be resolved before a stable public release.

Font exports do not grant rights to someone else’s outlines. Keep the required notices with fonts and components you redistribute.
